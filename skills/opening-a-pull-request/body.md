# Pull request body

Use these `##` sections, in order. Leave out a section with nothing to say.

- **Why**: the problem and the approach, in one to three sentences.
- **What changed**: one to three bullets.
- **Scope**: what this covers and what it leaves out on purpose.
- **Verification**: each check you ran and its result; mark mocked checks
  as mocked.

Keep the whole body under about 40 lines. Put long evidence in a linked
document, not in the body.
